Eunoia Prime

Eunoia Prime is a private, single-user personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computers. It uses the Google Calendar and Google Drive APIs, only for the owner's own Google account, to:

  • add appointments to the owner's Google Calendar, and
  • keep a backup folder of appointment documents in the owner's Google Drive.

It is not offered to the public and no one else's data is accessed.
